— ✦ —  ❀ Booking FAQs  — ✦ —

How do I book my cabin?

Use the Booking Link above — cabins are reserved through our travel advisor, Aligned Journeys, who will walk you through everything.

Can I book without a deposit?

A $250 per-person deposit is required to secure your cabin. Watch for Royal Caribbean's reduced-deposit promotions — our advisor will apply any that are running.

Are payments refundable?

Payment and cancellation terms follow the cruise line and Aligned Journeys booking agreement — the exact terms are shown during booking and may vary by fare type.

Can I upgrade my room later?

Room upgrades depend on availability and current pricing. Contact Aligned Journeys and they'll find you options.

What is the cancellation policy?

The cancellation policy is shown during booking and may vary by fare type. Aligned Journeys can explain the terms before you commit.

Can I invite my mom, my neighbor, or my favorite frenemy?

Absolutely! Invite your mom to help with the kids, bring another family from the hall so the kids can entertain each other, or invite a friend who would love a Caribbean getaway. We'd be happy for others to take advantage of our negotiated group rate and enjoy the cruise with you.

Just one important note: because our wedding events have limited capacity, only guests specifically named on the wedding invitation will be able to attend the ceremony and other private wedding activities. Your additional travel companions will still have an entire ship and a tropical vacation to enjoy!

Can I book excursions through you?

Excursions can be purchased through Royal Caribbean once your reservation is connected to your cruise account.

Do kids sail free?

Kids Sail Free and other promotions may be available depending on dates and eligibility — your advisor will apply the best eligible offer.
